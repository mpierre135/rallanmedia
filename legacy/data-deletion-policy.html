Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

Ideal-M respects your right to control your data.

1. Requesting Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by:

  • 📧 Emailing: support@rallanmedia.com
  • Subject line: "Data Deletion Request"

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • Email associated with your account
  • Confirmation you want your data deleted

2. What Happens After Deletion

Once verified:

  • Your account data will be permanently deleted
  • Stored personal information will be removed from active systems
  • Some data may remain in backups for a limited time for legal or security reasons

3. Processing Time

We typically complete deletion requests within 7–30 days, depending on system requirements.

4. Exceptions

We may retain limited data if required by:

  • Law
  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Financial recordkeeping

5. Confirmation

You will receive confirmation once deletion is complete.

6. Contact

For questions regarding data deletion:

📧 support@rallanmedia.com
